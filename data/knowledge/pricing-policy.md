---
## How the price is calculated
Parking is charged per hour. Every started hour is charged as a full hour, so a stay of 2 hours and 10 minutes is charged as 3 hours. The current hourly rate is part of the live facility data and is always taken from the database.

## Price estimate
The assistant shows an estimated price in Georgian lari (GEL) in the reservation summary. The estimate is informational; it does not collect a payment.

## Payment
Payment is made on site at the exit pay station by bank card or cash. Online payment is not available in this version.

## Overstaying
If you stay longer than your reserved period, the extra time is charged at the standard hourly rate, subject to availability. Please contact the attendant if you expect to be late.
