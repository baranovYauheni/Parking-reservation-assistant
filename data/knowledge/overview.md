## What the facility is
Demo Central Parking is a fictional covered parking garage used for demonstration purposes. It offers a small number of interchangeable parking spaces for cars. Spaces are not numbered for customers: a confirmed reservation guarantees a free space for the booked period, not a specific bay.

## Who can park
The garage is designed for passenger cars, small vans and SUVs. Motorcycles are welcome and use the same reservation process. Buses, trucks, trailers and caravans cannot be accommodated.

## Opening hours
The garage operates around the clock. The current opening hours, prices and the number of free spaces are published as live data by the parking system and may change; the assistant always reads them from the live database rather than from this document.

## Staff and help
An on-site attendant is present at the entrance booth. The attendant can help with entry problems, lost tickets and vehicle issues. Reservation requests are reviewed by a human administrator.
