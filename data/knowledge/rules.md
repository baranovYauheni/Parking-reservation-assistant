title: Parking rules and vehicle limits
---
## Vehicle size
The maximum vehicle height is 2.1 metres and the maximum length is 5.2 metres. Roof boxes and bicycle carriers count towards the height.

## Arrival and departure
You may arrive up to 15 minutes before your reservation starts. The space is held for the whole reserved period. Please leave the garage by the end of your reservation.

## Safety rules
The speed limit inside the garage is 5 km/h. Switch off the engine after parking. Smoking, refuelling and vehicle repairs are not allowed inside the garage.

## Lost ticket
If you lose your parking ticket, go to the entrance booth. The attendant will confirm your reservation using the car number.
