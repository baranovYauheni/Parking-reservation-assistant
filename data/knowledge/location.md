## Address
Where is the parking? Demo Central Parking is located at 12 Example Avenue in the Demo District of Tbilisi. The address is fictional and used only for this demonstration.

## How to get there by car
Drive along Example Avenue towards the city centre. The entrance ramp is on the right side, directly after the pedestrian crossing, and is marked with a large blue "P" sign. The exit leads onto Sample Street.

## Public transport
The nearest metro station, Demo Square, is a five-minute walk from the garage. City buses stop at the Example Avenue stop next to the entrance.

## Entrance and height
The vehicle entrance has a barrier and a clearance height of 2.1 metres. Pedestrians use the separate staircase and the lift next to the entrance booth.
