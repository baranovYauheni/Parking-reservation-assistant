---
## What data we collect
For a reservation we collect your first name, last name, car number and the reservation period. This data is used only to process the reservation.

## Who can see your data
Your reservation details are visible to you in your own chat session and to the parking administrator. The assistant never discloses details of other customers' reservations.

## How data is stored
Reservation data is stored in a private database and in the reservation register for approved bookings. It is never added to the public knowledge base that the assistant searches.
