## Can I choose a specific space?
No. All spaces are interchangeable, and the garage guarantees a free space for the reserved period rather than a particular bay.

## Can I change or cancel a submitted request?
Changing or cancelling a request that was already submitted is not supported in the chat in this version. Before you confirm, you can edit any field of your draft or cancel it. For a submitted request, speak to the attendant.

## What happens if my request is rejected?
A rejected request does not hold a space and is not recorded. You can submit a new request for a different period.

## Can I book for another car at the same time?
Yes, a reservation is per car. The same car cannot have two active reservations whose periods overlap.

## Does a pending request guarantee a space?
No. Only an approved reservation holds a space. Availability is checked again at the moment of approval.
