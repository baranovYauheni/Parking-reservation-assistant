title: How to reserve a parking space
---
## Starting a reservation
You can reserve a space in this chat by saying that you want to book. The assistant asks for your first name, last name, car number, and the start and end of the reservation. You can give the details one by one or several at once, for example "car: AB123CD".

## Date and time format
Enter dates as YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM in local Tbilisi time, for example 2026-10-20 14:00. You can also write "tomorrow 09:00". The start must be in the future, the end must be after the start, and a reservation lasts between 30 minutes and 30 days.

## Review and confirmation
Before anything is sent, the assistant shows a summary with the estimated price and current availability. You can change any field by writing, for example, "edit car number", or cancel the draft. The request is sent only after you confirm.

## Administrator approval
Every confirmed request is reviewed by a human administrator, who approves or rejects it. While the request is waiting, it does not hold a space. When it is approved, the system checks availability again and allocates the space.

## Final confirmation
After approval, the reservation is recorded in the reservation register. Your booking is complete only when the status shows that it was recorded. You can ask "what is the status of my reservation?" at any time in the same chat.
