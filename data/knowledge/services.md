## Electric vehicle charging
Two spaces are equipped with Type 2 electric vehicle chargers. Charging spaces are allocated on arrival and cannot be reserved in advance.

## Accessibility
Spaces close to the lift are kept for drivers with reduced mobility. Tell the attendant on arrival. The lift connects all levels with the street.

## Security
The garage has 24-hour video surveillance, good lighting and an on-site attendant. Valuables should not be left visible in the car.

## Other facilities
There are bicycle racks next to the pedestrian entrance and public restrooms on the ground floor.
